# Lore Audit Resolution Guide

Guidelines for automatically or interactively applying fixes from `/rg-lore-audit` directly into Obsidian notes.

## 1. Canon Inconsistencies (The Chronicler)
- Check `WORLD_CANON.md` for historical dates, lineage, and NPC relationships.
- Prefer diegetic reconciliation over deletion: introduce secret alibis, disguise magic, false rumors, or lineage revelations.
- Ensure all mentioned canonical entities are wrapped in `[[WikiLinks]]`.

## 2. Rule Balancing & DCs (The Rules Lawyer)
- Re-calculate monster attack bonuses: $\text{Ability Mod} + \text{Proficiency Bonus}$.
- Normalize check DCs:
  - DC 10: Routine under pressure
  - DC 15: Moderate challenge for trained adventurers
  - DC 20: Hard / heroic
- Add legendary actions or minions to solo boss encounters to fix action economy.

## 3. Player Agency & Mysteries (The Player Advocate)
- If a mystery hinges on a single clue, add two alternative discovery paths (Three-Clue Rule).
- Replace forced capture or surrender cutscenes with dynamic consequences for defeat or retreat.

## 4. Table Chaos & Utility Exploits (The Murderhobo Specialist)
- Add natural counters for common spell exploits (*Pass without Trace*, *Fly*, *Silence*) such as low ceilings, scent-tracking hounds, or magical wards.
- Provide contingencies if critical NPCs are killed or captured immediately.

## 5. Sensory Immersion & Boxed Text (The Sensory Immersionist)
- Trim read-aloud boxes to 2-3 sentences.
- Add lighting, acoustic, or scent anchors.

## 6. Safety Tools (The Safety Officer)
- Strictly excise any content violating campaign Lines.
- Soften graphic descriptions of Veil topics into quick fade-to-black transitions.
