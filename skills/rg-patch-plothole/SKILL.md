# Plothole & Lore Contradiction Patching (`rg-patch-plothole`)

`rg-patch-plothole` is the diegetic debugger of the world. Instead of clumsy out-of-game apologies or jarring rewrites ("Sorry guys, forget what I said last week"), this skill invents plausible, intriguing in-universe explanations that turn accidental continuity errors into deliberate dramatic mysteries.

## When to Use

- When the DM or players notice a contradiction between past sessions and current lore.
- When `/rg-lore-audit` flags a P0/P1 continuity clash.
- To resolve impossible timelines, conflicting NPC deaths, or rule-defying events.

## Principles of Diegetic Retconning

1. **Never "A Wizard Did It" (Unless Specific & Foreshadowed):** Cheap handwaves damage player trust. Solutions must feel intentional and grounded in the world's established rules and secrets.
2. **Turn Mistakes into Mystery Hooks:** An error is an opportunity. If a bandit opened a crypt only royal blood could touch, the bandit may unknowingly be an exiled royal bastard!
3. **Respect Established Player Perceptions:** Preserve what the players *saw* with their own eyes. Re-interpret what they *inferred* or what NPCs *claimed*.

## The 5 Diegetic Patch Strategies

1. **Unreliable Narrator & Propaganda:** The old source was lying, misinformed, or projecting state propaganda.
2. **Impostor, Doppelganger, or Magic:** Disguise self, changelings, *Simulacrum*, or magical projection.
3. **Hidden Lineage or True Identity:** The character possessed secret heritage, a concealed pact, or a mantle of power.
4. **Altered Geography or Magical Distortion:** Planar bleeding, forgotten subterranean portals, or temporal eddies.
5. **Two Distinct Entities:** A common name shared by two historical figures, or a title inherited across generations.

## Execution Workflow

1. **Isolate the Contradiction:** Read the conflicting notes in the vault and identify the exact incompatible facts.
2. **Brainstorm 3 Plausible Explanations:**
   - Rank them by dramatic weight, player impact, and narrative fit with `CAMPAIGN_FRAMEWORK.md`.
3. **Select & Integrate:**
   - Present the 3 options to the DM.
   - Upon selection, update the relevant lore notes in the vault (`World/NPCs/`, `WORLD_CANON.md`).
   - Add discovery clues for players to uncover the truth in future sessions.
