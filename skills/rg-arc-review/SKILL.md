# Campaign Arc & Ecology Review (`rg-arc-review`)

`rg-arc-review` evaluates macro-level campaign design across multiple sessions, modules, or expansive dungeon complexes. While `/rg-lore-audit` examines individual session notes or statblocks, `rg-arc-review` zooms out to inspect overarching narrative momentum, dungeon ecosystem plausibility, and faction interaction webs.

## When to Use

- When planning a multi-session adventure arc (e.g., Level 5-8 arc).
- When designing mega-dungeons or multi-level subterranean environments to verify ecology and internal politics.
- To audit faction front progress and long-term narrative payoff.

## Audit Dimensions

### 1. Narrative Pacing & Dramatic Escalation
- Are escalating stakes telegraphed early enough to create dread or excitement?
- Is there sufficient contrast between intense tension and quiet breathing room (town visits, downtime, travel)?

### 2. Dungeon Ecology & Inhabitant Politics
- **Resource Plausibility:** Where do monsters get food, fresh water, and air?
- **Territory & Faction Tensions:** If goblins and troglodytes live on the same level, why haven't they wiped each other out? What uneasy truce, geography, or common predator keeps them balanced?
- **Resting Sanctuaries:** Are there plausible defensible spots for party rests, or does the dungeon react as an active hive to alarms?

### 3. Faction Agendas & Escalation Clocks
- Do the 2-4 major factions pursue conflicting, proactive goals that progress regardless of party action?
- Are faction victories or setbacks visibly reflected in the world?

### 4. Regional Transitions & Clue Networks
- Are travel transitions between regions supported by environmental lore and rumors?
- Do discovery nodes point cleanly toward the climax without single-path bottlenecks?

## Output Report

Generate a macro-level review in Markdown covering:
- **Arc Strengths:** High points of tension and player choice.
- **Ecological & Structural Gaps:** Inconsistencies in dungeon factions, supply lines, or travel pacing.
- **Escalation Tuning:** Actionable adjustments to faction timers and climactic encounters.
